# Dusk Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information **Dusk** (“the Bot”) may collect and how that information is used.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled on a Discord server, Dusk may process:

* Discord user IDs
* Discord server IDs
* Channel IDs
* Role IDs
* Usernames and display names
* Bot configuration settings
* Moderation-related information
* Invite and server-related information required for bot features

Dusk does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, or Discord account login credentials.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

* Provide and operate Dusk's features
* Manage server configurations
* Perform moderation functions
* Maintain logs when configured by server administrators
* Track bot-related settings and functionality
* Improve reliability and performance

## 3. Data Sharing

Dusk does not sell personal information.

Information processed by Dusk may only be accessed or disclosed when necessary to operate the Bot, provide its features, comply with legal obligations, or protect the Bot and its users.

## 4. Data Retention

Data may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Dusk's features or maintain required functionality. Server administrators may request deletion of stored server-specific data.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Dusk operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Dusk does not control Discord's handling of information.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Dusk is not intended to independently collect information from children. Users must comply with Discord's age requirements and policies.

## 7. Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information processed by Dusk. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Dusk after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Dusk development team through the official support channel or server.

By using Dusk, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
